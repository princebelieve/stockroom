FROM REGISTRATION TO YOUR FIRST SALE.

Get your business running.

1

Register your business

Enter the business name and owner email in Stockroom. We generate a one-use registration key and email it to you; use it to complete setup. Referred businesses should open the promoter's link first.

2

Install & sign in

On Android or Windows, the owner connects each new device with their owner email and password, then staff sign in. In a new browser or PWA, staff open the business-specific sign-in link from Team management and use their own username and password; the owner does not need to enroll each browser. First sign-in and downloading business data need internet. Each browser profile keeps its own business workspace.

3

Work offline. Sync when ready.

After sign-in and the first data download, use the saved workspace offline. Sales and changes stay on that device until it reconnects and syncs. Sign-in on a new device, adding a device, and downloading fresh business data need internet.

Prepare your catalogue

Import your own CSV product list, use workspace starters or save a supplier reference catalogue for offline barcode lookup. Keep existing products; review selling units, costs, prices and stock.

Configure and practise

Use the owner setup checklist. Select the business timezone, staff permissions and printers. Practise sales, split payments, returns, closing cash and offline recovery before handing over a till.
