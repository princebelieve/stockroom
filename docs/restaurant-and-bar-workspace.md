# Restaurant & bar workspace

In **Business settings > Business type > Payment screens**, choose **Restaurant & bar only**. For a mixed business, keep your existing payment-screen choice and check **Enable separate Restaurant & bar workspace**, then **Save payment screens**.

This adds its own navigation screen, menu, table setup, bill sessions and orders. Fast food and supermarket screens keep their existing operations. Existing products can be linked explicitly to packaged menu offerings or recipes; payment-only receipt descriptions remain independent of stock.

## Everyday use

1. Open **Restaurant & bar**. The owner or administrator uses **Set up tables and seats** to add named tables and their seat counts. Occupied tables cannot be renamed, removed or resized. Use **Move to another table** to change seating while preserving the bill.
2. In **Menu**, add meals and drinks, prices and extras. Choose **Kitchen** or **Bar** for preparation tickets. Use **Recipes** for per-portion ingredients and extras, or **Packaged stock** for bottled drinks linked to a stock product.
3. Select a free table and enter its guest count, or use **Open bar tab** to name a customer/group without a table. Each opening creates a new bill session. This device remembers the selected bill when you reload or switch screens; it never substitutes a later customer's session at the same table.
4. Add menu items to a **New order**, selecting a seat or **Whole table / shared**. **Send for preparation** saves that round on the open bill. Submit another round whenever guests order more. Menu price changes do not alter saved rounds.
5. Use **Orders** or **Preparation** to start preparation, mark **Kitchen ready** and **Bar ready** independently, then **Mark served** when the round is ready. **Mark ready** confirms that all stations are ready. Restaurant orders may be served before payment. Kitchen and bar tickets contain only that station's lines, table/tab name, seat and preparation note. This routes printed content; choose the printer through the existing print controls. It does not automatically connect or buy another printer service.
6. The bill lists all non-cancelled rounds, descriptions, quantities, prices, seats and payment status. **Print itemized bill** prints a bill for review; an unpaid bill is clearly identified as unpaid.
7. Use **Settle remaining bill**. Choose all remaining items, one seat, particular items and quantities, or an equal share of the remaining bill. Shared items have their own seat choice. **Take payment** on a round opens the same payment screen for that round. Each saved payment creates **one receipt for all selected items**, even across multiple rounds. Cash supports change; transfer/terminal methods require an externally confirmed provider/reference. **Cash / card / transfer together** records several payment parts on the same receipt, including cash handed over and change. This records payments already received; it does not initiate a bank or terminal charge.
8. Saved order prices, tax, discounts and rewards are apportioned across partial quantities. Rounding cents stay on the remaining bill so all portions reconcile to the original total. Different loyalty accounts are paid separately. Payment, receipt, bill allocation and outbound sync records save in one transaction. An interrupted response can be retried using the same payment entry without duplicating money or stock deductions. Reload restores unfinished payment entry or its already-saved receipt. If items or payments change, review the updated bill and select **Refresh payment entry**. Preparation progress alone does not interrupt payment.
9. **Move to another table** transfers occupancy to a free table with enough seats. **Merge into another bill** combines an unpaid source bill into another open bill on the same till and currency, frees the source table and appends its numbered seats after the destination seats. Both bills retain their original order records and prices. A source bill with saved payments cannot be merged; settle its remaining amount separately. The destination must have enough seats or be a named bar tab.
10. **Close bill and free table** is available once every round is served or cancelled and every non-cancelled round has a saved payment. Closed sessions remain available for bill reprinting after a table is opened again.

## Corrections, costs and devices

Unpaid queued rounds can be corrected on their original till, with a reason. Merged source rounds preserve their original seats; cancel an incorrect source round and add its replacement on the combined bill. Later cancellations retain their history. Paid unserved rounds must be refunded in full before cancellation. Expand **Restaurant payment receipts** to reprint or refund selected quantities from a saved receipt, including a receipt covering several rounds. Existing returns handle refunds; ingredients already cooked are not restored. Recipe ingredients and linked packaged drinks are consumed when restaurant preparation starts, using the existing stock and cost operations. Later payment never deducts them again. Cancelling an unpaid served round requires an owner or administrator and retains consumed stock/cost. Refunds do not automatically restore prepared food or drinks; return unopened goods through an explicit stock adjustment when appropriate.

Orders and payment settlement use the till that opened the bill. Preparation progress can be shared through the existing synchronization workflow; recipe preparation still starts on the original till. Before using shared tables, synchronize and use one till to manage each open bill. Different offline devices cannot see each other's newly opened tables; conflicting occupancy is rejected during sync for owner review.

Use **Sync tables and bills** to share records. No new paid service or sync subscription is introduced. All participating apps and the existing sync backend must support `restaurant-v2` as well as `counter-v3`; update that backend before sharing restaurant records. Older servers leave uploads queued, and older clients are prevented from downloading restaurant records they cannot handle.

## Verification and practical limits

Automated checks cover consolidated and partial settlement, cash change, mixed payment methods, saved tax/discount reconciliation, repeat requests, failed-save rollback, receipt recovery, separate station readiness, table moves and merging. Browser checks cover desktop operation and offline browser receipt persistence. Actual printer output and Android hardware still need testing with the business's devices; printed station tickets use the existing printer controls.

Use one original till to manage each bill and synchronize before sharing table operations. The app cannot show another device's unsynchronized offline activity. Sync conflicts preserve records for owner review; recording a payment here never independently charges a customer's bank. This workspace handles restaurant and bar sales; hotel stays, bookings, nightclub admission and reservations require separate workflows.
