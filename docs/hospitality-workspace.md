# Hotel and nightlife operations

Implementation review: 2026-10-17. This guide has been checked against the current UI and server code. Hotel operations provide room and stay workflows, room transfers, housekeeping, work orders, folios, automatic accounting projections, a chronological business-date close, and a manual double-entry journal/trial balance, alongside separate Nightlife event workflows. This is not yet a complete PMS or event-venue management suite. The operational inventory and remaining standards gaps are recorded below so product claims match the implementation.

The Available workspaces screen has separate **Hotel operations** and **Nightlife operations** switches. They can be used independently or together. Both open the **Hospitality operations** destination and are covered by one Hospitality subscription entitlement. When both are enabled, the destination has Hotel stays and Nightlife events tabs. An Overview link names the enabled mode; the shared workspace remains the same capability.

## Hotel workflow

1. Add rooms with a room type, bed configuration, maximum guest count, amenities, base nightly rate and optional reservation deposit policy. A deposit can be a percentage of the stay total or a fixed amount; a fixed deposit is capped at the quoted stay total. Room types and amenities are free-form so each property can use its own categories. Keep room status current (Available, Maintenance or Inactive). Add non-overlapping date-bounded special-rate periods when seasonal or event pricing is needed; the start and end dates are both charged nights.
2. Book a stay against an active room, guest count, check-in date and check-out date. Select a saved customer profile when one exists; the stay and Guest folio link to that account and use its saved name and phone. The app rejects bookings above the room’s guest limit and overlapping room stays, snapshots the applicable per-night rates and deposit requirement, and shows stays in a monthly room-availability calendar. Invoices group nights at the same rate.
3. Assign a stay manager from staff who have Hotel operations permission. The Housekeeping board summarizes ready, dirty and in-progress rooms, assigned staff, scheduled cleaning and upcoming stays. Each room has an editable cleaning checklist. Check tasks off as linen is changed, beds are made, bathrooms cleaned, supplies replenished, bins emptied and the room inspected; Stockroom marks the room ready after every task is complete. Checkout starts a fresh checklist automatically.
4. Use Maintenance work orders to describe room faults, set priority and due date, assign a hotel-authorised technician, and track each repair through Open, In progress, Completed or Cancelled. Enter actual repair cost when updating the order. Creating an order marks the room as under maintenance; completing or cancelling all active orders returns a maintenance room to Available. Completed repair costs appear in the hotel ledger as repair expense and trade payable entries; there is no linked vendor invoice/payment workflow.
   Use **Out-of-order periods** on the room to schedule a dated closure when the room must be removed from inventory only for a specific interval. The end date is the first available date. These periods conflict with new stays, group holds, transfers and imported channel dates, appear on the availability calendar and Stockroom's exported iCalendar feed, and can only be cancelled with the history retained. They cannot be scheduled over an existing stay or tentative hold; resolve those bookings first.
5. Create a stay invoice through **Payments & receipts > Jobs & invoices**. Record the configured deposit or full payment there; each payment produces its own receipt. If the cashier has an open Register session on the current till, the payment is linked to that session and cash portions are included in its expected cash at close. Check-in is blocked until the room is ready and the required deposit has been collected. Check-out requires the stay invoice and all extra folio invoices to be fully paid. A checked-out room is marked dirty for housekeeping.
6. While a guest is checked in, add a priced folio charge or charge a stocked product. A stocked-product charge uses the saved product price and deducts branch stock when the invoice is created; collect payment through Jobs & invoices.
7. To transfer a checked-in guest, use **Transfer guest to room** on the stay. The destination must be active, ready, large enough and free for the remaining stay dates, including connected calendar blocks. The guest's contracted stay rates remain unchanged. The vacated room is marked dirty and receives a new cleaning checklist; the transfer is recorded in the stay history.

### Guest folios

Each stay begins with a Guest folio. Before or during the stay, add named payer folios (up to ten) such as Company or Guest. Route the accommodation invoice and each new incidental/stock charge to a selected folio. The stay shows billed, paid and due totals by folio while Jobs & invoices continues to retain each invoice and receipt as its own transaction. Existing charges cannot yet be moved between folios, and folio balances cannot be settled as one merged invoice/payment.

When a restaurant reservation is linked to a hotel stay, staff with Restaurant, Hotel operations and Jobs & invoices access can transfer unpaid table-bill items to one of that checked-in guest's folios. Stockroom creates an invoice linked to those selected items using the bill's saved total and tax, records the transfer on the table bill, and leaves the folio invoice due for collection. The same items cannot be charged twice, and hotel checkout still requires all folio invoices to be paid. This requires an active shared connection; it is not available for an unlinked bar tab.

### Group bookings

Use **Group booking** to reserve two to 50 rooms for the same dates under one group/company reference. Enter guests and counts per room or import a CSV rooming list using the downloadable template, then provide a shared contact. The app creates individual room stays atomically, so each retains its own room assignment, check-in, folio billing and checkout while showing the common group reference. Room dates and guest limits are checked before saving, and cloud sync checks room overlap again. **Change group dates and rooms** lets staff reassign guests across active rooms while moving every group stay to the same dates. The app checks room capacity, duplicate assignments, existing stays, tentative holds and connected calendar blocks; it recalculates nightly rates and deposit requirements and records each changed room assignment. All stays must still be booked and every estimate/invoice must be cancelled first. **Cancel entire group** has the same booked-only and folio-clear prerequisites. A single master group folio is not implemented.

Use **Tentative room holds** to temporarily block one to 50 active rooms for a group while its reservation is being confirmed. Holds conflict with guest bookings and connected iCalendar dates, appear on the availability calendar and exported room feeds, and expire automatically after one to 168 hours. Staff can release or archive a hold early, or convert a hold of two or more rooms directly into a group reservation. Conversion reserves every held room exactly once, assigns a named guest and guest count to each room, and releases the hold in the same local transaction; cloud sync checks the matching group and release together. Group date and room changes are coordinated across all stays; a single master group folio is not implemented.

Use **Front desk shift handoff** to open one branch shift, record opening context, submit the outgoing summary and outstanding items to an assigned hotel-operations staff member, then require that person or a manager to acknowledge. A branch cannot open another shift while the current shift is open or awaiting acknowledgment. This operational handoff log does not open/close cashier tills or reconcile cash and card settlements.

## Hotel ledger and accounting summary

Hotel-authorized staff can post a balanced journal from the Hotel ledger panel. Posted entries cannot be edited or deleted; correct an error with a correcting journal. The panel shows the current branch's trial balance, recent entries, and month-to-date income summary. Hotel business dates follow the business profile's configured reporting time zone across the UI, local server and cloud validation. Accommodation and event invoices, payments, recorded refunds and cancellation reversals generate system entries. Room revenue is recognized by stay night when that business date is closed; event admission revenue is recognized at guest check-in. Recorded expenses, stocked folio costs and completed repair costs also post. The Night audit panel presents a branch-specific review of due arrivals/departures, in-house stays, physical-room assignments, accommodation folios, front-desk handoffs and open folio balances. It blocks unresolved operational exceptions, requires dates to close in order, and saves a branch-scoped close snapshot. Shared Register activity remains a separate cashier reconciliation and is not automatically signed off by this audit. The account list is built in, and the ledger has no accounting-period close, settlement reconciliation or complete financial statements.

7. Booking-channel calendars are optional. A business owner, administrator, or staff account with Hotel operations access can open **Booking-channel calendars** on each room and connect that business's Airbnb, Booking.com, or other compatible listing by entering its private HTTPS iCalendar (.ics) URL. Then choose **Connect and sync**. Stockroom checks for updates about every 15 minutes and offers **Refresh now** and **Disconnect**. Imported date blocks are included in the room's Stockroom calendar feed and online availability checks; the cloud also rejects overlapping staff bookings when they sync. A device without a network connection cannot check partner dates until it syncs.
8. To share Stockroom bookings with a channel, create the room's Stockroom link and paste it into that channel's calendar-import settings. The feed publishes reserved dates only, without guest names or contact details. Revoking the link takes effect on the cloud feed after the change syncs; connected channels can keep cached dates until their next refresh. Calendar links can grant access to availability, so keep them private.
9. For Airbnb, get the listing's calendar export URL in **Calendar > Availability > Connect calendars**. Add it in Stockroom as an Airbnb calendar. Then copy the Stockroom room link and add it to Airbnb through **Connect to another website**. Airbnb says imported calendars refresh automatically about every three hours and can be refreshed manually. iCalendar links exchange availability dates. To synchronize reservations, guest details, rates, changes and cancellations, Stockroom needs a direct connection through the booking provider's approved partner API.
10. Other services can connect through this screen when they provide a compatible HTTPS iCalendar URL. Booking.com offers a direct [Connectivity API](https://developers.booking.com/connectivity/docs) for software providers; that requires partner onboarding, property connections and enabled API access. It is separate from iCalendar and cannot be activated until Stockroom has provider access.
11. The **Full channel-manager connection** screen supports a transitional business-owned Channex API-key mode and a Stockroom-managed credential mode. In managed mode, Stockroom creates a Channex property for the selected Stockroom branch and provisions/maps active room types and rate plans. Hotel staff enter the property's contact/address details in Stockroom, then connect the hotel's own Airbnb, Booking.com and other channels using Channex's property-scoped setup. OTA passwords stay in Channex; Stockroom never lists its shared integration account's properties. Configure an eligible `CHANNEX_API_KEY` with property-creation permissions and optionally `CHANNEX_ACCOUNT_EMAIL` on the server. Existing business-owned API-key connections remain supported. Stockroom imports revisions and publishes 365 days of availability, rates and consistent stay restrictions for mapped types. This is not certified; provider onboarding and operational validation remain prerequisites.

## Nightlife workflow

1. In **Event spaces**, register each room or hall and its guest capacity. Then create an event with a start and end time, optional registered space, setup and teardown buffers (up to 24 hours each), guest-facing description and operations plan. A registered space is reserved for the full buffered window; overlapping scheduled events are rejected locally and during cloud sync, and event capacity must be positive and fit the space. Events without a selected space can be used for off-site or unregistered venues; their schedule does not reserve a Stockroom space. Set a General ticket price and optionally add a separately priced VIP type with its own ticket limit. The event limit applies across all ticket types. Use the plan for the run of show, setup notes and staff handoffs. Assign an event host and door-staff lead. Assigned staff must have Nightlife operations permission in the active branch.
2. Add guests to the event list, classify each as General, VIP, Promoter or Regular guest, and select an admission ticket type. Customer-portal guests can select General or VIP tickets. When event or ticket-type capacity is full, booking requests join the waitlist and do not consume admission capacity.
3. Promote a waitlisted guest when a place opens; the app issues the QR pass and emails the guest when an address is saved and cloud email is configured. Staff can mark expected guests as no-shows after an event starts. For a paid portal admission, Stockroom creates the linked invoice and opens Paystack checkout when online collection is configured. Staff can also create an admission invoice and collect payment in Jobs & invoices. Free entries do not need an invoice.
4. Scan the guest's hospitality QR pass to check them in, or use the staff check-in action. Passes are single-use and checked against the active event and branch.
5. Customers can submit a private event request from the Nightlife portal with branch, event type, preferred date, guest count, email, phone and notes. Review requests in **Private event inquiries**, update the status, and add proposal terms and a quote subtotal. Applicable invoice tax is calculated in Jobs & invoices. **Create estimate for customer** makes a linked estimate. Convert an accepted estimate to an invoice there to collect a deposit or balance. Guests can pay an issued invoice online when Paystack is configured. Stockroom does not collect digital signatures or send inquiry-status messages.

6. If **Tables & tabs** is also enabled, event-linked customer table reservations and venue tabs appear with the event. The existing restaurant/bar workspace continues to own table layouts, menu orders, bill service and payment.

## Customer booking and QR passes

The customer portal has Hotel and Nightlife entry modes. Guests can browse room types by stay dates and guest count or upcoming events, reserve an available room type, review the assigned physical room, and present its QR pass for staff check-in. Room-type bookings allocate an active compatible room after rechecking existing stays, holds, maintenance closures and imported calendar blocks under the business sync lock. A paid Nightlife admission creates a linked invoice; when Paystack is configured, the portal opens hosted checkout immediately and Stockroom verifies the provider status, amount, currency and reference before posting payment. Guests can also pay other issued hospitality invoices online from **Your bookings**. Otherwise staff issue invoices and record payments in Jobs & invoices. Nightlife event-table booking is available only when Tables & tabs is enabled; food, drinks and tab settlement stay with venue staff.

These hospitality booking and hosted payment endpoints require internet access. Staff-side stay/event records and invoices use the app's existing branch, staff permission, synchronization and service-job systems; offline changes on another device are not visible until synchronized. Keep the original till for existing payment and invoice restrictions. See [Paystack setup](paystack-terminal.md) for merchant secrets and webhook configuration.

## Scope limits

The built-in workflows include dated room-rate periods, live stay quotes, housekeeping checklists, maintenance work orders, optional iCalendar availability exchange, registered event spaces with capacity and buffered non-overlapping reservations, tiered General/VIP admission, promo codes, custom attendee forms, paid ticket checkout, capacity-aware waitlists, bottle-service contents linked to bar menu stock, minimum-spend enforcement, cancellation/no-show/waitlist emails, and private-event proposals and invoices. The hotel ledger posts hospitality invoices, payments, refunds and cancellation reversals, general expenses, inventory use on stocked folio charges and completed repair costs. It also supports manual balanced journals, a trial balance, and monthly operating summary. A branch-scoped night-audit worksheet checks due arrivals, departures, in-house stays, room assignments, accommodation folios and front-desk handoffs, shows receivable warnings, requires dates to close in sequence, and snapshots room revenue and outstanding folio balances. Room revenue is recognized by stay night at close. This is an operational audit foundation, not yet a complete accounting or cashier/night-audit package. Hotel invoice payments made during an open shared Register session are linked to that cash session, and cash portions count toward its expected cash. Staff with Register permission can review hotel cash receipts/refunds by session alongside the full drawer's expected, counted and variance totals, and identify hotel cash receipts lacking a session. Register close does not automatically post its variance to the hotel ledger or reconcile card and bank settlements. Configurable hotel account/tax mapping, accounting-period locks, full financial statements and statutory reports are not implemented. iCalendar exchanges availability dates only; direct provider reservation/rate synchronization requires provider-approved access, credentials and certification. The current product does not ship a certified full-sync connector for a booking provider. This guide does not claim compatibility with every hotel's or event centre's statutory, payment, channel, or reporting requirements. Staff assignments reuse existing staff identities and branch permissions; there is no separate hospitality staff directory.

## Large-property standards inventory

This is the target matrix for the hotel upgrade, not a claim that every item is already implemented. A large property also needs cashier controls, a full night audit, complete group workflows, guest/company profiles, advanced room-move controls, package controls, service posting, department reporting, access review, a business date, and supported integrations. Reference comparisons here use [Oracle OPERA reservations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/hospitality/opera-cloud/25.1/ocsuh/ch_reservations.htm), [OPERA cashiering and finance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/hospitality/opera-cloud/25.1/ocsuh/c_cashiering_finance.htm), and [OPERA end-of-day procedures](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/hospitality/opera-cloud/23.2/ocsuh/c_endofday_procedures.htm); these are representative capability references, not a universal legal standard.

### OTA connection ownership and target

The hotel or hotel group owns its Airbnb, Booking.com and other distribution accounts and remains responsible for its listings, channel terms and rates. Stockroom operates as the hosted PMS using one centrally configured Channex integration identity and creates a separate Channex property for each Stockroom hotel branch. Hotel staff provide that property's contact/address details in Stockroom; Stockroom creates its property, room types and rate plans, and maps each active Stockroom room type automatically. This follows Channex's PMS onboarding guidance to create properties, room types and rate plans over its API. The account is shared at the integration layer, while each Channex property is mapped to exactly one Stockroom business/branch and is never listed to other tenants. Staff then connect the hotel's own OTA accounts inside Channex's property-scoped channel setup. Stockroom does not collect OTA passwords or use a shared Airbnb/Booking.com listing. Existing encrypted business-owned Channex API-key connections remain available when the central credential is not configured. The managed flow requires `CHANNEX_API_KEY` to have property-creation permissions and still needs provider onboarding, property-authorization review, and operational certification.

After Stockroom creates and maps the property, hotel owners or authorized staff can launch Channex's embedded channel setup from Stockroom. Stockroom mints a one-time token limited to that property's channel page and filters setup to Airbnb and Booking.com; Channex documents these channel codes and says its iframe is limited to channel management for the supplied property. The token expires after 15 minutes. This allows staff to initiate OTA connections from the Stockroom workspace without entering OTA credentials in Stockroom. It remains a Channex-mediated integration, not a direct Airbnb/Booking.com API connection. See [Channex Channel IFrame](https://docs.channex.io/api-v.1-documentation/channel-iframe).

Today, **Booking-channel calendars** stores a business-owned private iCalendar URL per room and publishes a separate Stockroom date feed. This remains availability-only. The Hotel operations workspace supports a transitional business-owned API-key connection, or Stockroom-managed access when `CHANNEX_API_KEY` is configured. In managed mode Stockroom creates one Channex property per Stockroom branch, creates/maps active Stockroom room types and manual rate plans, and persists partial onboarding progress so staff can resume after a provider error. It does not expose the shared account's property list. Each property can belong to only one Stockroom business and branch. The hotel connects its Airbnb, Booking.com and other channel accounts inside Channex; Stockroom does not collect those OTA passwords. Stockroom polls Channex booking revisions, imports new reservations, applies changes and cancellations where they do not conflict with checked-in stays or active folios, and acknowledges a revision only after saving it. An unhandled revision stays unacknowledged and its error appears in Hotel operations for resolution. Stockroom also sends a 365-day availability and rate snapshot for mapped room types at one-minute intervals; availability accounts for active stays, holds, room closures and connected calendar blocks. If rooms mapped to a channel room type have different prices for the same date, Stockroom closes that channel inventory for the date and surfaces the rate conflict. Minimum/maximum stay, closed-to-arrival, closed-to-departure and stop-sell restrictions now publish with room-type rates when every physical room agrees; inconsistent restrictions close channel availability and raise a visible warning. Two-way ARI and reservation sync remain uncertified. Multiple currencies are not supported for imported stays; mismatched currencies require resolution. Channex API-key access requires an eligible account and property-creation permissions; the server operator must configure integration access. The managed integration still needs Channex provider onboarding and operational certification.
