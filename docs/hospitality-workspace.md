# Hotel and nightlife operations

The Available workspaces screen has separate **Hotel operations** and **Nightlife operations** switches. They can be used independently or together. Both open the **Hospitality operations** destination and are covered by one Hospitality subscription entitlement. When both are enabled, the destination has Hotel stays and Nightlife events tabs. An Overview link names the enabled mode; the shared workspace remains the same capability.

## Hotel workflow

1. Add rooms with a room type, bed configuration, maximum guest count, amenities and base nightly rate. Room types and amenities are free-form so each property can use its own categories. Keep room status current (Available, Maintenance or Inactive). Add non-overlapping date-bounded special-rate periods when seasonal or event pricing is needed; the start and end dates are both charged nights.
2. Book a stay against an active room, guest count, check-in date and check-out date. The app rejects bookings above the room’s guest limit and overlapping room stays, snapshots the applicable per-night rates, and shows stays in a monthly room-availability calendar. Invoices group nights at the same rate.
3. Assign a stay manager from staff who have Hotel operations permission. The Housekeeping board summarizes ready, dirty and in-progress rooms, assigned staff, scheduled cleaning and upcoming stays. Each room has an editable cleaning checklist. Check tasks off as linen is changed, beds are made, bathrooms cleaned, supplies replenished, bins emptied and the room inspected; Stockroom marks the room ready after every task is complete. Checkout starts a fresh checklist automatically.
4. Use Maintenance work orders to describe room faults, set priority and due date, assign a hotel-authorised technician, and track each repair through Open, In progress, Completed or Cancelled. Enter actual repair cost when updating the order. Creating an order marks the room as under maintenance; completing or cancelling all active orders returns a maintenance room to Available. Work orders are retained on the room record; they do not create expenses or vendor payments.
5. Create a stay invoice through **Payments & receipts > Jobs & invoices**. Record deposits or full payment there; each payment produces its own receipt. Check-in is blocked until the room is ready. Check-out requires the stay invoice and all extra folio invoices to be fully paid. A checked-out room is marked dirty for housekeeping.
6. While a guest is checked in, add a priced folio charge or charge a stocked product. A stocked-product charge uses the saved product price and deducts branch stock when the invoice is created; collect payment through Jobs & invoices.

7. Booking-channel calendars are optional. To import dates, open **Booking-channel calendars** on the room, enter a channel name and its private HTTPS iCalendar (`.ics`) URL, then choose **Connect and sync**. Stockroom checks for updates about every 15 minutes and offers **Refresh now** and **Disconnect**. Imported date blocks are included in the room's Stockroom calendar feed and online availability checks; the cloud also rejects overlapping staff bookings when they sync. A device without a network connection cannot check partner dates until it syncs.
8. To share Stockroom bookings with a channel, create the room's Stockroom link and paste it into that channel's calendar-import settings. The feed publishes reserved dates only, without guest names or contact details. Revoking the link takes effect on the cloud feed after the change syncs; connected channels can keep cached dates until their next refresh. Calendar links can grant access to availability, so keep them private.
9. For Airbnb, get the listing's calendar export URL in **Calendar > Availability > Connect calendars**. Add it in Stockroom as an Airbnb calendar. Then copy the Stockroom room link and add it to Airbnb through **Connect to another website**. Airbnb says imported calendars refresh automatically about every three hours and can be refreshed manually. iCalendar links exchange availability dates. To synchronize reservations, guest details, rates, changes and cancellations, Stockroom needs a direct connection through the booking provider's approved partner API.
10. Other services can connect through this screen when they provide a compatible HTTPS iCalendar URL. Booking.com offers a direct [Connectivity API](https://developers.booking.com/connectivity/docs) for software providers; that requires partner onboarding, property connections and enabled API access. It is separate from iCalendar and cannot be activated until Stockroom has provider access.

## Nightlife workflow

1. Create an event with a start time, optional total capacity, guest-facing description and operations plan. Set a General ticket price and optionally add a separately priced VIP type with its own ticket limit. The event limit applies across all ticket types. Use the plan for the run of show, setup notes and staff handoffs. Assign an event host and door-staff lead. Assigned staff must have Nightlife operations permission in the active branch.
2. Add guests to the event list, classify each as General, VIP, Promoter or Regular guest, and select an admission ticket type. Customer-portal guests can select General or VIP tickets. When event or ticket-type capacity is full, booking requests join the waitlist and do not consume admission capacity.
3. Promote a waitlisted guest when a place opens; the app issues the QR pass and emails the guest when an address is saved and cloud email is configured. Staff can mark expected guests as no-shows after an event starts. For a paid portal admission, Stockroom creates the linked invoice and opens Paystack checkout when online collection is configured. Staff can also create an admission invoice and collect payment in Jobs & invoices. Free entries do not need an invoice.
4. Scan the guest's hospitality QR pass to check them in, or use the staff check-in action. Passes are single-use and checked against the active event and branch.
5. Customers can submit a private event request from the Nightlife portal with branch, event type, preferred date, guest count, email, phone and notes. Review requests in **Private event inquiries**, update the status, and add proposal terms and a quote subtotal. Applicable invoice tax is calculated in Jobs & invoices. **Create estimate for customer** makes a linked estimate. Convert an accepted estimate to an invoice there to collect a deposit or balance. Guests can pay an issued invoice online when Paystack is configured. Stockroom does not collect digital signatures or send inquiry-status messages.

6. If **Tables & tabs** is also enabled, event-linked customer table reservations and venue tabs appear with the event. The existing restaurant/bar workspace continues to own table layouts, menu orders, bill service and payment.

## Customer booking and QR passes

The customer portal has Hotel and Nightlife entry modes. Guests can browse available rooms/dates or upcoming events, create a guest booking, review the booking, and present its QR pass for staff check-in. A paid Nightlife admission creates a linked invoice; when Paystack is configured, the portal opens hosted checkout immediately and Stockroom verifies the provider status, amount, currency and reference before posting payment. Guests can also pay other issued hospitality invoices online from **Your bookings**. Otherwise staff issue invoices and record payments in Jobs & invoices. Nightlife event-table booking is available only when Tables & tabs is enabled; food, drinks and tab settlement stay with venue staff.

These hospitality booking and hosted payment endpoints require internet access. Staff-side stay/event records and invoices use the app's existing branch, staff permission, synchronization and service-job systems; offline changes on another device are not visible until synchronized. Keep the original till for existing payment and invoice restrictions. See [Paystack setup](paystack-terminal.md) for merchant secrets and webhook configuration.

## Scope limits

The built-in workflows include dated room-rate periods, live stay quotes, housekeeping checklists, maintenance work orders, optional iCalendar availability exchange, tiered General/VIP admission, promo codes, custom attendee forms, paid ticket checkout, capacity-aware waitlists, bottle-service contents linked to bar menu stock, minimum-spend enforcement, cancellation/no-show/waitlist emails, and private-event proposals and invoices. Direct OTA reservation and rate synchronization uses approved provider APIs and requires Stockroom's partner access and property credentials. Digital signatures, ticket-marketplace discovery, hospitality-specific membership programs and a hotel accounting ledger are separate opportunities for future development. Staff assignments reuse existing staff identities and branch permissions; there is no separate hospitality staff directory.
