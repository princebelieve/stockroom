# Hotel and nightlife operations

The Available workspaces screen has separate **Hotel operations** and **Nightlife operations** switches. They can be used independently or together. Both open the **Hospitality operations** destination and are covered by one Hospitality subscription entitlement. When both are enabled, the destination has Hotel stays and Nightlife events tabs. An Overview link names the enabled mode; the shared workspace remains the same capability.

## Hotel workflow

1. Add rooms with a nightly rate. Keep room status current (Available, Maintenance or Inactive).
2. Book a stay against an active room, guest, check-in date and check-out date. The app rejects overlapping room stays and shows stays in a monthly room-availability calendar.
3. Assign a stay manager from staff who have Hotel operations permission. Room housekeeping can separately be marked Ready, Needs cleaning or Cleaning in progress, assigned to hotel-enabled staff and given a scheduled time.
4. Create a stay invoice through **Payments & receipts > Jobs & invoices**. Record deposits or full payment there; each payment produces its own receipt. Check-in is blocked until the room is ready. Check-out requires the stay invoice and all extra folio invoices to be fully paid. A checked-out room is marked dirty for housekeeping.
5. While a guest is checked in, add a priced folio charge or charge a stocked product. A stocked-product charge uses the saved product price and deducts branch stock when the invoice is created; collect payment through Jobs & invoices.

## Nightlife workflow

1. Create an event with a start time, optional capacity, admission price, event host and door-staff lead. Assigned staff must have Nightlife operations permission in the active branch.
2. Add guests to the event list. Guest entries inherit the event admission price. A guest cannot be admitted beyond the event capacity.
3. Create an admission invoice for a paid entry and collect its balance through Jobs & invoices before checking the guest in. Free entries do not need an invoice.
4. Scan the guest's hospitality QR pass to check them in, or use the staff check-in action. Passes are single-use and checked against the active event and branch.
5. If **Tables & tabs** is also enabled, event-linked customer table reservations and venue tabs appear with the event. The existing restaurant/bar workspace continues to own table layouts, menu orders, bill service and payment.

## Customer booking and QR passes

The customer portal has Hotel and Nightlife entry modes. Guests can browse available rooms/dates or upcoming events, create a guest booking, review the booking, and present its QR pass for staff check-in. A signed-in customer account can link the booking to that customer's wallet and show invoice balances. The customer portal does not collect hotel or admission invoice payment directly: business staff issue invoices and record payments in Jobs & invoices. Nightlife event-table booking is available only when Tables & tabs is enabled; food, drinks and tab settlement stay with venue staff.

These hospitality booking endpoints require internet access. Staff-side stay/event records and invoices use the app's existing branch, staff permission, synchronization and service-job systems; offline changes on another device are not visible until synchronized. Keep the original till for existing payment and invoice restrictions.

## Scope limits

This is a connected operating workflow, not a full hotel PMS or nightclub ticketing/accounting platform. It does not currently provide room-rate/channel management, housekeeping task history or workload board, automated online payment collection for customer bookings, ticket marketplace integration, membership programs, or a general hospitality accounting ledger. Staff assignments reuse existing staff identities and branch permissions; there is no separate hospitality staff directory.
