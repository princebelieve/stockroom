# Stockroom: capabilities, user problems and expectations

Reviewed 2026-10-08 against the current frontend, shared business handlers, local storage/sync adapters, public feature page and workspace guides. This review describes implemented workflows; it does not certify every production service or hardware connection. Existing owner-reported printer/scanner tests remain valid evidence and are distinguished from automated tests.

## Product promise

**Keep daily business moving through internet outages, using a workspace that fits how you sell, with stock, payments and people connected.**

The value is continuity and control: a cashier can finish supported local work, an owner can understand money and stock, and a business can choose an appropriate workflow without buying into unrelated operational complexity. Stockroom has five selling workspaces: Product sales, Oil sales, Payments & receipts, Order counter, and Tables & tabs. Jobs, church collections, online orders and production extend those workflows; catalogue industry labels are not additional complete industry systems.

Lead with offline operation and workspace choice. Then show the particular user's daily problem: a shop's stock and cash; a printer's invoice and deposit; a takeaway's preparation; a restaurant's open bill. Avoid introducing every configuration option before registration.

## What to advertise, and where

The skippable introduction now has seven illustrated, single-caption benefits: offline trading after setup; workspace choice; sales/payment receipts; stock/purchasing; preparation; customer/supplier balances; sales/cost/staff visibility. It gives a useful product picture without turning daily screens into a brochure.

Use the [public features page](../features.html) for fuller explanation, including service jobs, measured/bulk selling and material/yield tracking. Use the workspace pages for audience-specific examples. Keep operational instructions under Help and put validation at the action it affects.

Suggested campaign examples, all tied to implemented work:

- **Retail:** “Keep checkout moving through internet outages. Track what sold, what is left, and what suppliers are owed.”
- **Services/printing:** “Quote the job, record the deposit, track the balance, and issue a receipt for each payment.”
- **Takeaway:** “Send the order to preparation and keep payment, preparation and collection clear.”
- **Restaurant/bar:** “Keep rounds on one bill and settle the whole bill or selected portions.”
- **Oil/wholesale:** “Use measured quantities, configured packs and customer or bulk prices.”
- **Owner:** “Review sales, stock costs, cash differences and staff activity from recorded business data.”

Avoid “everything works offline,” “instant synchronization everywhere,” “automatic verification of every transfer,” “complete accounting,” “all printers/scales supported,” and “a complete system for every industry.” These promises exceed the implementation.

## Improvements driven by the promise

These are proposed acceptance targets, not features implemented by this review. Prioritize reliability and the first useful transaction over adding more industry labels.

| Priority | Expectation and present gap | Concrete acceptance target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0: offline/sync trust | A saved local record must be distinguishable from an uploaded record. Setup changes legitimately queue. The latest fix labels them and stops calling a failed connection ready; the reported phone fetch failure is still not diagnosed. PWA intentionally avoids background cloud pulls that lock local storage. | On the actual phone, identify the failing endpoint/request and resolve its deployment, connection or browser restriction. Demonstrate queued settings and a sale surviving offline reload, then one successful upload with no duplicate receipt. Show last successful sync and a concise, actionable retry state. Do not promise automatic PWA reconnection upload without implementing and measuring it. |
| P0: first useful transaction | Registration and three saved setup steps exist, but selecting a workspace does not create actual items, menus or tables. An owner can still land in a configured but unusable workspace. | In each selling workspace, guide a new owner to create the minimum real item/service/menu/table, enter the applicable price/cost, complete a transaction and view its receipt. Existing users enter daily work directly. Optional printer/staff/advanced setup can wait. |
| P1: shared-device recovery | “Works across devices” creates expectations of safe stock, wallet, refund, reservation and open-bill handling. Offline devices do not share a lock; sync-version compatibility and original-till ownership matter. | Exercise two disconnected tills, concurrent stock/wallet/refund work, stale bill changes, reconnect and lost-till recovery. Preserve completed receipts, explain the discrepancy and provide a safe review outcome. Verify the deployed server supports current capabilities. |
| P1: money/cost confidence | The app already warns about missing costs, but “see profit” can be read as a complete accounting promise. External payments are often manually confirmed. | A sample trading day reconciles deposits, balance payments, split tenders, refunds, supplier debt, cash count, material consumption and expenses without double counting. Cost completeness is visible alongside the estimate. Manual confirmation remains explicit. |
| P1: device/platform acceptance | Desktop, offline PWA and Android use related but different adapters and printing paths. Owner-reported physical tests cover specific hardware, not every combination. | Record a practical support matrix with connection type, device model, app version and actual results. Repeat the intended workspace journey on real phones and Windows, including keyboard/touch behavior, storage failure, printer failure and recovery. |
| P2: remote visibility | Owners may expect live alerts and history even while disconnected. Push/email need connectivity; the notification inbox lacks a durable offline cache after a fresh launch. | Cache previously loaded inbox records if offline-history access is a product requirement. Label the last update time and delay; verify enabled channel delivery on real devices. |

## Delivery in this change

Expanded the introduction and public feature copy, and created this capability-to-problem review. No new accounting, hardware, payment-processing or synchronization guarantee is introduced. The implementation evidence and acceptance targets are intended to govern future advertising and product work.
