# Stockroom Business: AI video brief and tester invitation

## Product points to lead with

1. **Keep everyday work moving through internet outages.** After setup and the first business-data download, owners can keep recording supported sales and work on-device offline. Changes sync automatically when the connection returns, and the owner can also sync on demand.
2. **Choose the workspaces your business uses.** Product sales, Oil sales, Payments & receipts, Order counter, Tables & tabs, Hotel operations and Nightlife operations. Hotel and Nightlife are distinct workflows within Hospitality: hotels manage room bookings, housekeeping, maintenance and guest folios; nightlife teams plan events, manage General/VIP admissions, coordinate the run of show and staff, and handle private-event inquiries.
3. **Connect stock to checkout.** Scan product barcodes live with the device camera or scanner, add the matching item to a sale, take payment, and keep stock current as the sale is recorded. Supermarket workflows add supplier orders, partial deliveries, returns, wastage and optional expiry tracking.
4. **Support different ways of selling.** Measured oil sales, service jobs/invoices with deposits and balances, counter orders and restaurant tabs. Hotel teams manage room availability, seasonal rates, guest arrivals, group bookings, housekeeping, repairs and guest folios. Nightlife teams plan events and venue schedules, manage capacity, General/VIP tickets, guest lists and waitlists, private-event quotes, QR admissions and door check-in; event tables, bar orders and tabs connect to Tables & tabs.
5. **A free workspace with practical value.** Payments & receipts is always free. Owners can record non-stock payments, issue or share receipts, keep payment history, and manage service jobs/invoices with estimates, deposits and later balance payments. It helps organize service income and customer payment records even when the business does not use inventory checkout.

Also feature owner controls: individual staff accounts and permissions, branches, customer and supplier records, cash register, business-date reports, stock and expense views, and encrypted Windows backups.

## Business types and first setup

First-time setup lets an owner choose a business type, select a selling workspace if needed, and enable Hotel operations, Nightlife operations, or both directly. Hotel-only and Nightlife-only setups are supported, and hospitality can be combined with selling workspaces. Owners can review suggested catalogue or menu entries for the selected selling workspace. Currency, reporting timezone and payment methods can be set during setup; staff and devices such as scanners and printers can be configured as the shop is prepared. Business type suggestions cover printing and copy shops; general retail, grocery, supermarkets and wholesale; measured oil; restaurants, takeaways, catering, bakeries, food production and drinks; hotels and nightclubs; pharmacies and personal care; clothing, electronics and appliances; construction, automotive, agriculture, furniture, office and school supplies, books, hardware, event supplies, bulk goods and services. Owners can combine workspaces to fit the business they actually run.

## Workspaces to bring to life

- **Product sales:** scan or search a catalogue, build a basket, take payment, issue a receipt and keep stock current. Stock tools include receiving, supplier orders and balances, partial deliveries, pack conversions, stock counts and transfers, price tiers, returns, wastage, batch costs and optional expiry tracking.
- **Oil sales:** sell measured quantities or configured containers using customer and bulk prices.
- **Payments & receipts:** always free; record non-stock charges, print or share receipts, search payment history, and track jobs from estimate to completion, deposits and balances. Church collections adds donor, pledge and contribution records for churches.
- **Order counter:** take counter, takeaway and online customer orders; send work through preparation; track readiness and collection; use recipes for ingredient deduction; and manage menus and extras.
- **Tables & tabs:** serve restaurant and bar tables or named tabs, add order rounds, route preparation, move or merge bills, manage event-linked reservations and settle whole bills or selected seats/items.
- **Hotel operations:** configure rooms, occupancy, deposits and dated rates; handle individual, group and customer-portal bookings; coordinate arrivals, check-in, guest room transfers, housekeeping and maintenance; manage guest and group folios, accommodation and incidental invoices, payments, room revenue and business-date night audit. Hotel ledger and trial-balance views help owners follow property activity.
- **Nightlife operations:** schedule events and venue spaces, plan operations, assign hosts and door staff, set General/VIP ticket tiers and promo codes, manage admissions, capacity and waitlists, offer online booking and scan guest QR passes. Build private-event proposals, event packages and bottle-service offers; connect event reservations, bar orders and tabs to Tables & tabs.

The same setup supports many business types through suggested starting catalogues and configurable fields, while workspaces provide the daily operating tools. Lead the story with what owners can do in the chosen workspace combination.

## Referral programme

Stockroom includes referral tools for business owners and a separate promoter programme. An owner can copy a business invitation link from **Subscription → Referral rewards**, then see verified rewards, balances and payout history in the referral wallet. Promoters can create an account, copy a tracked link, follow completed business registrations and verified rewards, and manage payout details or requests. Referral credit is tied to the referred business redeeming its registration key; reward credit is earned from successful subscription payments, not link clicks or account creation. The programme can reward up to four successful payments for each referred business.

Add a quick referral scene to the ad: an owner copies their invitation link, a new business follows it through setup, then the owner views referral activity and the rewards wallet. If speaking about reward percentages or showing example earnings, use the current configured rates and label figures as examples. The app’s settings distinguish owner referral rates from visitor/promoter rates, so do not merge those programmes or invent a universal percentage.

## How to explain Payments & receipts to business owners

**Short version:** “Payments & receipts is Stockroom’s always-free workspace for non-stock payments and service income. Create and share clear receipts, keep payment history, and use Jobs & invoices to carry work from estimate through deposits to final balance.”

**What it does:**

- Record descriptive items, quantities and prices for walk-in or service payments.
- Record cash, bank transfer, POS Terminal, split payment and (when enabled) customer wallet, with provider and payment reference details.
- Print or share receipts; search payment history; reprint receipts; record eligible refunds while retaining the original receipt.
- Use optional Jobs & invoices to move from estimate to invoice, track work progress, receive deposits and later payments, and follow unpaid/overdue balances.
- Record actual stock materials used on an issued job when relevant; this is separate from the payment itself.
- Use the separate Church collections tab for funds, donors, pledges, contributions, refunds and donor statements when configured.

**Why owners use it:** It gives service income and other non-stock charges a consistent receipt and payment trail; helps answer “what was this payment for?”; makes deposits and remaining balances easier to follow; and keeps these records available in Stockroom’s always-free workspace. Estimates, issued invoices and received payments each have a clear place in the job history.
