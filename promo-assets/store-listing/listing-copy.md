# Google Play listing copy

**App name:** Stockroom Business

**Short description (66/80 characters):**

Manage sales, services, churches, hotels, restaurants & nightlife.

**Full description:**

Stockroom Business helps you manage daily work for shops, service businesses, churches, hotels, restaurants and nightlife venues. Choose the workspaces that fit your business.

• **Sales and stock:** build a product catalogue, scan barcodes, record sales, manage stock and suppliers, track expenses, and keep customer records.
• **Services and jobs:** prepare estimates and invoices, record deposits and later payments, and issue receipts for services and other non-stock charges.
• **Church collections:** organize funds, donors and pledges, record contributions, and prepare donor statements.
• **Hotel operations:** manage rooms and bookings, guest arrivals, housekeeping, maintenance and guest folios.
• **Nightlife events:** schedule events, set General and VIP admissions, manage guest lists and waitlists, and check guests in with QR passes.
• **Food and hospitality:** take counter orders and manage restaurant tables, tabs and bills.
• **Team and reports:** set staff permissions, manage branches, and review business activity in reports.

After setup and the first data download, supported work continues on the device when you are offline. Changes sync automatically when the internet connection returns.

Payments & receipts is always free. Other features depend on the workspace and subscription selected for your business.
