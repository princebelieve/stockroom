This policy describes how S. B. Ibhadode Technologies (“we”, “us”) handles information through Stockroom Business, its website, browser app, Windows app, Android app, visitor promoter accounts, and related support. It reflects the features currently provided.

In brief: Stockroom keeps business work on the device for offline use. When cloud sign-in or sync is used, the information needed for accounts, syncing, subscriptions, referrals, and support is sent to our service providers. We do not store customers’ full payment card details.

Information we handle

  • Business accounts: owner and staff names, owner email, staff usernames, business identifier, password verifier, sign-in and device enrollment records.
  • Business workspace: products, categories, prices, stock, sales, order and void details, customers and customer wallet entries, expenses, staff roles, and settings that a business chooses to enter. Some workspace data is stored on the device and may be synchronized to the cloud when sync is configured and available.
  • Subscriptions: plan, amount, currency, transaction reference, payment status, access dates, and related support records. Paystack processes the payment. Stockroom does not receive or store full card numbers or card security codes.
  • Visitor promoter and referral records: promoter name and email, password verifier, referral code and attributed registrations, earned rewards, withdrawal requests, and payout status. If a promoter adds a bank account, the account details are sent to Paystack to create a transfer recipient. Stockroom stores the recipient code and limited display details such as the account name and last four digits.
  • Notifications: in-app notices about subscriptions and referral rewards. If you enable browser push, Stockroom stores your browser push endpoint and encryption keys so it can deliver those notices to that browser. You can turn push off from the notification bell or your browser settings.
  • Messages and support: information in support requests, registration-key email delivery, password-reset messages, and related delivery status. Gmail API is used to send supported account and registration emails.
  • Technical information: authentication tokens, device identifiers, sync operation identifiers, request and security logs, and basic information sent when your browser or device connects to the service.

How we use information

We use information to run Stockroom, keep accounts secure, store and synchronize business records when enabled, process subscriptions and referral rewards, send requested account emails, provide support, prevent abuse, troubleshoot service problems, and meet legal obligations. Business data is not used by the Developer Control Centre to inspect a business’s sales or inventory; that centre manages subscriptions, access, registration keys, referrals, and payouts.

For customer and employee records entered by a business, that business decides what information to enter and how it uses those records. The business is responsible for providing appropriate notices and having a lawful basis to collect and use them. S. B. Ibhadode Technologies handles those records to operate the Stockroom features the business chooses, including cloud sync where enabled.

Where information is stored and shared

Information can remain in app storage on the device, including browser storage used by the PWA. Render hosts the cloud API and MongoDB stores cloud records. Vercel delivers the website and PWA when deployed there. Browser push providers receive encrypted notification payloads and the endpoint needed to deliver them. Paystack hosts checkout and processes subscription payments and referral transfers; Stockroom receives transaction results and references needed to update access and wallets. Google Gmail API sends requested account and service emails. These providers receive information needed for their part of the service and handle it under their own terms and privacy notices. Provider infrastructure may process information in more than one country depending on deployment and service configuration.

We do not sell personal information. We disclose information when needed to operate the service, protect users and the service, comply with law, or respond to valid legal requests.

Local storage, sync, and security

Stockroom uses browser or app storage for sign-in state, settings, and the local business workspace. Offline changes remain on that device until they sync. Keep device access protected and sync or export records regularly. Clearing browser or app data can remove local records. Cloud sync is not a substitute for a separate backup.

We use access controls and security measures appropriate to the service, but no storage or network transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Contact us promptly if you believe an account or device has been accessed without permission.

Retention and your choices

We keep information while needed to provide the service, maintain account and payment records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. To close an account, sign in and use Close your account. Deactivation takes effect when the request is submitted. Permanent cloud deletion is scheduled after the waiting period displayed in Stockroom (90 days by default), and you can cancel before that date. Closing a business owner account also stops cloud access and sync for that business. Offline copies already saved on individual devices cannot be remotely erased. We may retain limited payment, fraud-prevention, or other records where the law requires it. You can also ask to access or correct personal information, request deletion or restriction, object to certain processing, or ask about portability by contacting us.

You can stop using a feature or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Some account, payment, or security functions may not work if the information needed for them is removed.

Children and third-party links

Stockroom is a business management product and is not designed to collect information directly from children. The service may link to third-party websites, including payment and support services; their privacy practices are governed by their own notices.
