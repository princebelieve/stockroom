STOCKROOM BUSINESS

Choose the workspace for your business

Start with a business preset, then enable the selling and management tools your business needs. Mixed businesses can combine workspaces.

Retail and supermarkets

Product checkout, barcode scanning, pack sales, stock counts, suppliers, customer accounts, returns, reports and online retail orders. Barcode and photo suggestions assist catalogue entry; owners supply their own prices and stock.

Wholesale and retail oil

Sell measured quantities or containers, use pack conversions and retail, bulk or customer pricing, and manage stock, supplier deliveries and customer accounts.

Fast food and takeaway

Menu items, extras, recipes, preparation queues, ingredient consumption, batch preparation and yields, preparation tickets, customer QR orders and delivery progress.

Restaurants, bars and lounges

Tables, seats, open tabs, rounds, kitchen/bar preparation, bill settlement and reservations. Copy menus, extras and recipes between restaurant and takeaway workspaces.

Services and printing shops

Reusable service items, estimates, jobs, invoices, partial payments, overdue filters and customer statements. Printing jobs can record material consumption and captured costs. Simple payment-and-receipt entry remains available.

Church payment collection

Record collections and donations, separate funds, donor statements and pledges through the church tools. Staff access remains owner-controlled.

Other stock-based businesses

Clothing, electronics, furniture, stationery, building supplies and other catalogue presets can use shared stock and selling tools. A preset configures the workspace; it does not create specialist room-booking or prescription workflows.
