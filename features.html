STOCKROOM BUSINESS

Business tools that work together

Sell, manage stock, serve customers and control staff access. Save everyday work on the device first and synchronize when connected.

Payments and receipts

Accept cash, bank transfer, external POS, split payments and owner-enabled customer wallets. Calculate change, retain payment references, print receipts and send or share digital receipts. Staff confirm external transfers; customers can use ordinary bank transfers.

Stock and purchasing

Track opening stock, supplier purchases, partial deliveries, pack conversions, supplier balances and returns. Record stock counts, wastage and optional batches and expiry dates. Import product lists and preserve existing products.

Staff access you control

Each staff member signs in with their own account. Owners choose access checkboxes for selling, stock, customers, jobs, preparation, reports and other tools. Owners can remove staff access while keeping business history.

Customer accounts

Keep customer records, prepaid wallets, owner-approved credit and customer statements. Optional birthday reminders reuse the app’s notification channels. Customers can view their wallet and online order progress.

Online orders and delivery

Publish selected retail products or food menus and share customer links or QR codes. Staff accept, prepare or pick, confirm payment, assign a delivery agent, dispatch and record completion. Configure fixed or delivery-area prices. Online requests need connectivity and staff availability confirmation.

Reports and closing cash

Review sales, payment evidence, returns, staff activity, stock value and estimated profit in the business’s reporting timezone. Open and close cash-register shifts and compare expected and counted cash. Cost warnings identify incomplete profit inputs.

Notifications and support

Use the notification centre, browser push, Android push and Windows desktop notifications where enabled. Existing notifications include operational and account updates. Email supports receipts and account messages; in-app support requests have offline drafts and a reference number.

Recovery and control of your data

Synchronize business records across enrolled devices and recover a lost till. Export supported reports and product data. Windows offers encrypted business backups, scheduled backups and restore controls.

Offline-first operation

After installation, initial sign-in and downloading business data, continue ordinary trading through internet outages. Cloud registration, new-device setup, online ordering, remote alerts and email delivery need connectivity. Keep local storage safe and synchronize regularly.
