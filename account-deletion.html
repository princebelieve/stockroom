Close your Stockroom account

You can request account deactivation and deletion from Stockroom. Sign in to confirm the request. Deactivation starts immediately; permanent cloud deletion is scheduled after the waiting period set by the Stockroom developer (14 days by default). The scheduled deletion date appears after the request is accepted.

Owner: sign in and request closurePromoter account

During the waiting period

  • You can cancel the request before the scheduled date by signing back in and opening Close business account as the owner (promoters use their wallet).
  • For a business owner, cloud access and sync for the whole business stop when the request is submitted. The business's Stockroom cloud records are deleted on the scheduled date.
  • Staff cannot request account deletion. Staff access is managed by the business owner. Promoters close their personal promoter account, not referred businesses.
  • Recurring subscriptions must be managed separately through their payment-provider controls.
  • Offline copies already stored on a Windows computer, Android device, or browser cannot be remotely erased. Remove Stockroom data from those devices separately.
  • Payment, fraud-prevention, and legally required records may be retained where the law requires it.

For help or if you cannot sign in, contact support@sbi.globalcreest.com or info@sbi.globalcreest.com. See the Privacy Policy for information about retention and data requests.
