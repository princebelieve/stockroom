WHAT WORKS WITHOUT AN INTERNET CONNECTION

Routine sales can continue
after setup.

After installation, initial sign-in and downloading your business data, reopen your saved workspace, record local sales and work with inventory, even in airplane mode.

Go online to register, set up another device, sync changes, manage cloud staff or pay for a subscription. Cached subscription expiry and grace-period rules still apply offline.

Keep your device and browser storage safe. Clearing app data removes the local workspace; sync and back up regularly.

A PRACTICAL STOCKROOM WORKFLOW

From first setup to the next day's sales

Use these steps to prepare a device before an outage and understand which Stockroom workflows still need a connection.

1. Set up while connected

Register the business, sign in, choose the workspaces it will use, and download the business data to the device. Add products, prices and opening stock before using Product sales. A business preset is a starting point; it does not automatically set your prices or stock.

2. Give each person the right access

Owners create individual staff accounts and choose which operations each person can use. For example, a cashier can be allowed to sell without receiving permission to change stock or business settings.

3. Keep routine work on the device

After initial sign-in and download, an existing workspace can record ordinary sales and inventory work during an internet outage. A configured printer or scanner may require its own device setup.

4. Reconnect and synchronize

When the connection returns, synchronize the device so its saved business records can be shared with other enrolled devices. Keep the device's local data safe and back it up; offline availability is not a substitute for a backup.

Requires an internet connection: first registration and sign-in, initial business-data download, adding or connecting another device, syncing, online customer orders, remote alerts, email delivery and online payment processing.

OWNER-CONTROLLED ACCESS

Separate selling access from stock control

Staff use their own sign-in. An owner chooses which operations each account can access, including product sales, cash register, purchasing, stock changes and reports. This lets a business assign day-to-day selling without giving every cashier access to business configuration.
